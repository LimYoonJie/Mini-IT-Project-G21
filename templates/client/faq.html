Accounts and verification

How do I create an account?

Select Account, choose Register, enter your details and MMU student email, then verify the one-time password sent to your inbox.

Why do I need an MMU student email?

Student email verification helps keep the marketplace within the MMU community and makes transactions more trustworthy.

What should I do if my verification code expires?

Use the resend option on the verification page to request a new code, then enter it before it expires.

How do I reset my password?

Open Account, choose Login, and select the password reset link. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

Buying

How do I buy an item?

Browse Products, open a listing, add it to your cart, and complete checkout. Contact the seller to agree on the handover details.

Can I buy more than one item?

Yes. Add each available item to your cart and review the cart before continuing to checkout.

How do I search for an item?

Open Products and search by name, category, or description. You can also filter by category and condition.

What happens after checkout?

Your order is recorded and you should coordinate a safe pickup or delivery arrangement with the seller.

Selling

How do I create a listing?

Choose Sell, sign in, provide the item name, category, price, condition, description, and image, then submit the listing.

Can I edit my listing?

Open your profile, select the listing, choose Edit, update the details, and save your changes.

How do I remove my listing?

Open your listing from your profile and choose Delete. Deleted listings are no longer available to buyers.

What makes a good listing?

Use clear photos, an accurate condition, a fair price, and a detailed description that mentions any defects.

Payments and handover

How does payment work?

Add the item to your cart, continue to checkout, and review your order before confirming. Contact support if you have a payment issue.

Does the marketplace provide delivery?

Buyers and sellers should agree directly on a suitable MMU pickup point or delivery arrangement after purchase.

Where should I meet for pickup?

Choose a public, well-lit MMU location that works for both people. Do not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

Safety and support

How do I report a seller or listing?

Open the listing, choose Report, select the reason, and include enough detail for the marketplace team to investigate.

What should I do if I suspect a scam?

Stop the transaction, keep relevant messages or receipts, and contact the marketplace team with the listing and seller details.

How do I contact the marketplace team?

Use the Contact link in the footer, complete the form, and send your message. The team will review it and reply to your email.
